# Privacy operations and retention procedures

The owner reports Nigerian privacy review completed. This document describes software operation, not legal approval. Use the actual reviewed notices, purposes, appropriate lawful bases, processor arrangements, response deadlines and contractual retention schedule. Do not invent registration/notice numbers or treat a checkbox as a universal legal basis.

## Records and access boundaries

- Applications remain department-restricted and separate from subscriptions/inquiries. Applicant accounts are not required. Internal evaluations/private notes never appear in applicant tracking or public privacy pages.
- Marketing requires separate optional selection and double opt-in. Notice/version, requested/confirmed timestamps and preference events preserve evidence. Applying does not subscribe anyone. Campaigns require Super Admin approval; recipient preferences are rechecked at send time. No open-tracking pixels are used.
- Contact/rights submissions acknowledge the configured notice, without implying consent is the basis for every use. Contact stores its notice snapshot/version. Rights requests retain the acknowledgment in private review history; email verification alone is not conclusive identity proof.
- Staff see assigned inquiries only, except Super Admin. Assignment is an explicit authorization to that inquiry. Reply text is separate from private notes and is sent only after a Send reply action.
- Public CMS actor metadata is independent of private casting decisions. An actor's email is not automatically linked to CMS posts; production erasure therefore requires explicit manual identity/contract/hold review.

## Access/deletion request workflow

1. The person uses `connect.php?view=rights`, verifies the emailed link by an explicit POST, or contacts the approved public privacy mailbox if verification is unavailable. The configured response-date reminder defaults to 30 days but must match the applicable reviewed deadline. It is not an automatic legal determination.
2. Super Admin reviews the request, scope, mailbox control and any proportionate extra identity evidence. Record only necessary evidence/rationale in private history. Do not copy full identity documents into free-text notes. Status choices distinguish Verified, In Progress, Resolved and Declined. Unverified requests cannot release or delete data through the tools.
3. For access, download the **staff-review** JSON with the current private code. It includes matching submitted applications, messages and subscription records and excludes internal evaluations/staff notes. Review third-party information/exemptions and arrange secure, separately reviewed attachment transfer. No export is automatically emailed to an applicant. Securely remove temporary review/export copies after completion.
4. For deletion, examine every matching application/contact/subscription, production record, external provider and backup location. The tool handles one record deliberately at a time; it does not imply one application deletion purged every record for the email. Do not change the application key to defeat identification.
5. Place a legal hold with a necessary documented reason where required. Holds are record-specific and block automatic eligibility for the irreversible anonymization action. Review/release them deliberately under policy; a hold is not a justification for indefinite retention without review.
6. Anonymization requires Super Admin, current private code, exact identifier/email, rationale and either a matching active verified deletion request or an expired applicable retention period. Selected cast/crew additionally require a verified request and explicit production/contract policy review; they are not ordinary unsuccessful-applicant retention candidates.
7. The application action changes its public reference/hash, removes identity/contact/answers, clears private applicant notes/audition snapshots, cancels bookings/revokes tokens and removes personal evaluation rows. It retains nonidentifying structural records/staff action history. Ordinary recruitment/evaluation actions refuse an anonymized application. Contact anonymization removes message/identity/reply-note content/assignment. Subscription anonymization removes the email/preferences and disables queued sends/tokens. Existing SMTP/provider copies cannot be recalled automatically.
8. Attachment rows are removed immediately so downloads cannot authorize them. Physical bytes enter a deletion queue. Use **Process file deletions**, at most 20 per action, until pending/failed counts are resolved. Backup start refuses unresolved jobs. Never manually weaken storage protection to make cleanup work.
9. For production CMS material, archive it first. The exceptional **Reviewed production content erasure** action requires a verified deletion request, manually reviewed subject relationship and contract/hold grounds, typed slug and private code. It scrubs all snapshots/SEO/private reveal metadata of that one archived item and replaces its slug. This intentionally overrides ordinary immutable-history retention under a reviewed privacy decision. Repeat for related approved records where warranted. Content Editors cannot do this.
10. Unreferenced optimized images can be removed separately by Super Admin/private code/exact image ID/reason. Any current or historical revision reference prevents removal. Review all references before erasure. This removes three optimized variants, not copies already downloaded, sent to a provider or stored in old backups.
11. Record actual completion, partial fulfillment, lawful refusal/exception or follow-up in the request history. Resolving a request alone does not perform deletion/export. Coordinate external processors and erase temporary/off-host copies according to the approved policy. Do not report full erasure if pending physical jobs/provider/backup actions remain.

## Retention review lists

These identify candidates; they never silently delete people. Use the explicit Initialize missing retention dates action for up to 100 non-selected applications whose role has closed or whose deadline passed. It fills only missing dates from the earlier closure/deadline under the existing 1–12 month policy and never extends established dates or revives anonymized records:

- Unsuccessful/non-selected applications: existing Milestone 3 dates, capped by configured 1–12 months from recruitment closure/deadline. Selected production records use the separate contractual policy/review.
- Contact inquiries: configured 1–365 days after first resolution/closure (default 180). Reopening does not silently extend the original review date; open inquiries are not automatically candidates.
- Unconfirmed subscriptions: seven days. Unsubscribed subscriptions: 30 days. Active subscriptions need actual ongoing lawful retention/consent review; no arbitrary automatic marketing expiration is inferred.
- Unverified privacy requests: seven days. Resolved/declined request records: review after 90 days from receipt. Active verified requests are not automatically purged before handling. Adjust the operational policy through review if legal obligations differ; holds can preserve necessary records.
- Encrypted host backup parts: configured 1–30 days, default seven, with bounded deliberate deletion. Off-host copies require separate deletion/custody records. Unused/orphan data outside the application's registered storage and external production systems need a supervised review.

The interface lists 20 due records per category and 50 hold/anonymization records; older controls/history remain stored. Work through candidates and revisit lists. Request/inbox/campaign lists paginate 20; selected histories display up to 100 entries. Access export is bounded to 500 matching records per category and requires supervised bounded export beyond that. Backup/privacy actions have no public endpoints or hidden background deletion jobs.

## Incident and recovery precautions

Use HTTPS and protected session/storage directories. Never share private invitation/preference links, API keys, private codes, access exports or applicant screenshots in support. Token-bearing initial URLs may occur in hosting/proxy logs even after clean redirects/no-referrer/no-store: restrict logs and apply the reviewed retention policy. SMTP/provider copies have their own retention; app tools do not delete them automatically.

After restoring an older snapshot, keep campaigns/recruitment/services paused until post-snapshot erasures, opt-outs, holds and administrator decisions are reconciled. Restoring a database is not permission to contact every old subscriber. See [recovery](recovery.md). Record failures, custodian actions and evidence, and conduct appropriate incident/data-protection review where required.
